How to Recognize Phishing

Phishing messages often try to trick you into revealing passwords, financial details, or other personal information.

  • Check the sender's email address carefully.
  • Be cautious of urgent threats or unexpected prizes.
  • Inspect links before opening them.
  • Never share passwords or one-time passwords through email.
  • Verify unusual requests using an official contact channel.

Remember: If a message feels suspicious, pause and verify it before taking action.
